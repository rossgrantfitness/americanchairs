Shipping, Returns & Warranty

DRAFT POLICIES: these will be finalized, and matched to each manufacturer’s terms, before we accept any orders.

Shipping

  • Chairs ship directly from the manufacturer in the U.S.
  • Many chairs are built to order. Lead times range from about 1 week to 12 weeks depending on the maker (for example, ERA chairs take about 8–12 weeks). Each product page will show its lead time.
  • Shipping cost will be shown before you pay. No surprises.

Returns

  • Unused chairs in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Opened, non-defective returns may carry a restocking fee of 15%–25%, matching the manufacturer’s fee. The exact fee will be shown on each product page before you buy.
  • Made-to-order or custom-upholstered chairs may be final sale. This will also be shown before you buy.
  • No restocking fee, ever, on defective or damaged chairs. We’ll make it right.
  • Please inspect your delivery and report any freight damage within 48 hours.

Warranty

Every chair carries its manufacturer’s warranty – often 5 to 15 years, including 24/7 use on intensive-use models. We handle the paperwork with the factory for you.
