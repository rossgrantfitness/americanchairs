About Us

Our Story

American Chairs is a small, American-owned business with a simple idea: people who sit for a living deserve chairs that are built to last – and built here at home.

Most “gaming chairs” are imported racing seats with thin foam and peeling faux leather. Meanwhile, American factories in Wisconsin, Texas, California and New York quietly build chairs for 911 dispatch centers that are rated for 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and for people up to 550 lbs. We think gamers, coders and remote workers deserve to know those chairs exist.

Our Promise

  • Honest labels. We use each maker’s exact country-of-origin wording. Period.
  • Heavy-duty first. We focus on chairs rated for 24/7 use and big & tall users.
  • Real people. When we open, your call or e-mail will be answered by a real person in the U.S.
  • Straight pricing. We follow each maker’s advertised pricing. No fake “90% off” sales.
