How to Read a “Made in USA” Claim

The Federal Trade Commission has strict rules about the words “Made in USA.” Here’s the plain-English version:

The label says…What it means
“Made in USA”“All or virtually all” of the product – parts, processing and labor – is from the U.S.
“Assembled in USA”The final, substantial assembly happened here, but some parts may be imported.
“Made in USA of U.S. and imported parts”A qualified claim: built here, with a meaningful share of imported content.

That’s why every chair on our site shows the maker’s own wording about where it is built, and why we never put flags next to imported products. Some famous “American” brands build many of their chairs in other countries – always check the country-of-origin label.
