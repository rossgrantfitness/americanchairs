# US-Made Heavy-Duty Office, Task and Gaming Chairs: Brands and How to Become a Reseller

*Researched November 17, 2026 from web search results. Manufacturer websites were blocked in this session, so "brand says" means a search result quoting the brand's own site or documents. "Third party" means a retailer, reviewer or directory. **Unverified** means not confirmed.*

## Summary: who to call first

1. **Concept Seating (Wisconsin): High.** It has the only published reseller agreement found that fits you: drop shipping allowed, sales on your own website allowed, and you qualify by meeting 3 of 4 easy criteria. Chairs are 550 lb, 24/7. Online-only stores (BTOD, Husky Office) already carry it.
2. **ERA Office Chairs (Cerritos, California): Medium-High.** Made-to-order, hand-upholstered, 400–500 lb, 24/7 chairs. Several online-only retailers sell them. No published dealer program, so call.
3. **Seating Inc. (Nunda, New York): Medium-High.** Built to order in Western New York, with 400 lb (24/7) and 550 lb lines and a 15-year warranty. Sold through dealers and online retailers.
4. **Neutral Posture (Bryan, Texas): Medium-High.** Its 500 lb, 24/7 Big & Tall chair is sold widely online (Office Depot, BTOD). One catch: after an FTC inquiry it dropped unqualified "Made in USA" claims, so word your listings carefully.
5. **BodyBilt (Navasota, Texas): Medium.** The brand says its chairs are made in Navasota, and it is the only small US maker found with real gaming chairs (G7; GX4 up to 500 lb). But it sells direct to consumers, and no dealer program is published.

**Legal note for your listings:** Under FTC rules, an unqualified "Made in USA" claim means "all or virtually all" US content, and that applies to you as a reseller too. Use each brand's exact wording (for example Haworth's "Assembled in the USA") and keep a copy of it on file.

**Wholesaler back door: mostly closed.** In early October 2025 Essendant said it is leaving the independent office-products dealer channel. S.P. Richards (SPR) is now the main national wholesaler for independents and runs a furniture drop-ship program from 7 US warehouses. Its brand list is **unverified**; HON is confirmed there but is not reliably US-made.

## Brand details

### Concept Seating: High
- **Where made:** HQ Brookfield, WI. The brand says chairs are "handcrafted in Milwaukee, Wisconsin." Owned by Laacke & Joys Company LLC (per a brand PDF).
- **Models:** **3150HR** operator chair: 550 lb, 6-year 24/7 warranty, 7-leg steel base, articulating adjustable headrest, Dymetrol seat suspension. Retail $1,957–$4,400. **3142R1** high back: 550 lb, 10-year frame warranty, optional neck roll, quick-change slip covers. **3156HR**: 800 lb bariatric. BTOD: "not built to be pretty."
- **Look (3150HR):** Black fabric-upholstered high-back operator chair with a thick, tall back, an adjustable headrest pad, wide padded arms, and a heavy black seven-leg steel base on casters.
- **Dealer program:** There is a public Reseller Agreement PDF (Dec 2022 version) and a Reseller Portal where you request access. Terms:
  - Non-exclusive.
  - Drop shipping is allowed, shipped FOB Brookfield.
  - Internet sales are allowed only on your own website. Amazon, eBay and discount sites are banned.
  - To qualify you meet 3 of 4 criteria: sell 15 chairs a year; buy a demo chair at 50% of reseller cost; promote your reseller status on your website; meet the service-provider criteria.
- **Online-only proof:** BTOD, Husky Office, Advan-Ergo. **Wholesaler:** none found.

### ERA Office Chairs (ERA Products): Medium-High
- **Where made:** Cerritos, CA (16440 Manning Way). California's "Made in California" manufacturer directory (CMTC) says seats are "cut, sewn, and upholstered by hand onto steel fabricated frames." Chairs are made to order, with 8–12 week lead times.
- **Models:** **Chairman 2-pc**: 500 lb, 26" seat, steel inner frame, seat slider, flip-up arms, 30" base, thick wrap-around headrest; ~$3,689–$3,989. **Big Sur**: 500 lb, 28" aluminum base (chrome or black) or optional 30" steel base. **Henry HEN-2PC**: ~$1,840 (BTOD 2024).
- **Look (Chairman):** Plush black leather two-piece executive chair with a separate pillowed back and an extra-wide seat, a thick wrap-around headrest, padded flip-up arms, and a wide black five-star base.
- **Dealer program:** None published (**unverified**). Call (562) 404-8111.
- **Online-only proof:** Husky Office, Office Chairs USA, Office Chairs Outlet, BTOD. Drop-ship: **unverified**.

### Seating Inc.: Medium-High
- **Where made:** Nunda, NY. The brand says chairs are "built to your order... at our factory in Western New York." Family-owned; SBA woman-owned certified.
- **Models:** "400 Super Duty / Intensive Use" line rated 400 lb for 24-hour, 7-day use. "550 Generous Fit" line rated 550 lb with a reinforced steel substructure and fixed arms. All carry a 15-year warranty. Examples: **Monterey II 550**, **Pearl II 550**. Prices and looks are **unverified** (quote-based).
- **Dealer program:** The brand says it sells "through office furniture dealerships" and lists reps, and that some models are on The Human Solution, Wayfair and Bizchair. Factory shipping charges are published, but whether they ship direct to your customers is **unverified**. Phone 1-800-468-2475.

### Neutral Posture: Medium-High
- **Where made:** Bryan, TX. A directory also lists a facility in Ontario, Canada. Older brand literature said "Made in the U.S.A." An FTC closing letter (exact date **unverified**; law-firm coverage suggests about 2018) says the company stickered over and removed unqualified "Made in USA" claims and told its dealers and retailers.
- **Model:** **Big & Tall 24/7 (BTC10100 and Jr. BTC16800)**: 500 lb, 27" seat (23" on the Jr.), steel-reinforced frame, 28" heavy-duty black base, oversized padded arms, optional headrest, 5-year warranty even in 24/7 use. Prices: $1,372 (Office Depot) to $2,075 (BTOD Jr.); pillow-top version $3,143.
- **Look:** Black fabric big-and-tall task chair with an extra-wide cushioned seat and large back, chunky padded arms, an optional headrest, and a broad black five-star base.
- **Dealer program:** Uses an authorized dealer network and a "Find a Rep" page. A public application was not found, so contact a rep or call 979-778-0502.
- **Online proof:** Office Depot, BTOD, ErgoDirect, The Human Solution, Advan-Ergo.

### BodyBilt (ErgoGenesis): Medium
- **Where made:** Navasota, TX. The brand says products are "made in the USA in Navasota, Texas" and "manufactured at our headquarters."
- **Models:**
  - **Captain 24/7 3500 Series:** 300 or 500 lb; Elmo Soft leather (black is the Quickship color); SKYDEX seat; Optima 24/7 arms with heavy-duty pads; black 27" Marquis base (polished chrome optional). About $3,728 at a retailer.
  - **GX4 Gaming:** the brand says it suits gamers up to 500 lb (Newegg says 300 lb, so confirm). Seat is 22% wider, with a contoured upholstered back. $1,033.
  - **G7 Gaming:** $899 (was $1,199). Newegg says "Proudly Made in the USA." Reviewers describe it as office-chair styled, with 4D arms.
- **Look (Captain):** Black leather executive chair with a contoured manager back, optional neck roll, heavy-duty T-arms with padded tops, and a black five-star base.
- **Dealer program:** A state-by-state business rep locator exists. A third party lists a 10% affiliate program (through the Impact network). A reseller program is **unverified**.
- **Online proof:** Newegg, The Human Solution, Advan-Ergo, Office Chairs USA. Its own direct-to-consumer site competes with you.

### OM Seating (formerly Office Master): Medium, if US origin is confirmed
- HQ and on-site operations are in Ontario, CA. The brand's corporate documents found here make no US-origin claim, so origin is **unverified**. Ask for a country-of-origin statement per model.
- **Maxwell MX85IU:** 450 lb, 24/7, 24" seat, ballistic-nylon back shell, 7-year warranty; ~$940–$1,500. The 24-Seven line is rated 300–350 lb.
- Sold widely online (Parlor City Furniture, ErgoDirect, officemasterchairs.com). Dealer application is **unverified**.

### Via Seating: Medium-Low
- Sparks, NV. The brand says "Our chairs are manufactured in Sparks, Nevada." Shipping records show components imported from China. Flokk (Norway) acquired Via in February 2025.
- **Brisbane HD 24/7:** 350–450 lb, mid back, polished aluminum low-profile five-star base, optional articulating headrest, optional ballistic nylon; 12-year warranty. Price **unverified** (sold through price books).
- Sells through contract dealers. Online sellers include Parlor City Furniture and Italcasa.

### Iron Horse Seating (United GP): unverified
- GSA price list: "IRON HORSE Seating products are MADE IN THE USA." Tested to federal intensive-use standard FNEW 83-269 for 24/7 use. Contact ironhorseseating@unitedgp.com or (847) 816-7100. Models, capacities and prices are **unverified**.

### Herman Miller (incl. Embody Gaming): Low
- The brand says its gaming chairs are "made in the U.S." The Vantum FAQ says "made in the United States." The plant is **unverified**.
- **Embody Gaming (with Logitech G):** 300 lb, 12-year warranty. New Ignite and Nova colorways list at $1,995; store pricing is $1,676–$2,395 with sales.
- **Look:** Slim skeletal back with a central black "spine" and flexible pixelated ribs under thin fabric; black with cyan accents (other colorways exist); height- and width-adjustable arms; no headrest; graphite die-cast aluminum five-star base.
- **Dealer program:** Dealers are recruited through regional Channel Development teams; a US application form is **unverified**. Gaming chairs sell through the brand's store and "select retail partners." Herman Miller sued online seller Madison Seating, so expect strict enforcement.

### Other big contract brands: Low (Low-Medium for Humanscale, SitOnIt, 9to5)
Haworth, Knoll, Allsteel, Humanscale, SitOnIt and 9to5 sell mainly through contract furniture dealers, which usually means a showroom and a sales territory (exact requirements **unverified**). Origin facts are in the table. Haworth builds Fern to order in Bruce, Mississippi. Humanscale enforces minimum advertised pricing and publishes a list of banned web sellers; you apply by opening a support ticket.
